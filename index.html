Account Services

We Handle Client Accounts & Remove Restrictions

Facing a suspension, hold or restriction on your Amazon or eBay account? Our specialists manage client accounts end-to-end and restore restricted accounts quickly and safely.

🛒

Amazon Account Handling

Complete A-to-Z management of your Amazon seller account — listings, orders, inventory, case logs and performance metrics, handled by our expert team.

🏷️

eBay Account Handling

Full eBay account management including store setup, listings, order processing, customer service and defect-rate control to keep your account healthy.

⚠️

Restriction Removal

We remove selling restrictions, category and brand gates, listing blocks and verification holds so you can get back to selling without downtime.

🔓

Suspension Reinstatement

Professional appeal writing and Plan of Action (POA) for suspended Amazon accounts and eBay MC011 / MC999 restrictions — with a proven recovery process.

🛡️

Account Health Protection

Ongoing monitoring of ODR, late shipment and policy metrics with preventive actions so your account stays safe, compliant and growing.

✅

Verification & Compliance

We guide and complete KYC, identity, bank and business verifications for Amazon, eBay and Shopify — fully compliant with marketplace policies.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does AYZ Success Hub offer?
We offer Amazon, eBay and Shopify services, website development, digital marketing, SEO, social media ads, 24/7 camera monitoring (UK & USA), plus a full training academy covering eCommerce, web development and freelancing.
Which countries do you serve?
We serve clients in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, UAE and across Europe, with remote teams and 24/7 support.
Do you offer free consultation?
Yes. Book a free consultation through the contact form or WhatsApp and we'll analyze your business and recommend the best strategy.
How does the camera monitoring service work?
Our professional operators monitor your CCTV cameras live, 24/7. We provide alarm response, event and intrusion detection, incident reporting, and night monitoring for construction sites, warehouses, commercial buildings and retail stores.
Are the training courses beginner-friendly?
Absolutely. Courses are structured from beginner to advanced levels, with live classes, recorded sessions, real projects, mentorship and a certificate on completion.
Do I get a certificate after a course?
Yes, every academy course includes a completion certificate plus portfolio projects you can show to clients or employers.
How long does a website project take?
Landing pages typically take 1–2 weeks, business websites 2–4 weeks, and larger e-commerce or custom platforms 4–8 weeks depending on scope.
What technologies do you build with?
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, PHP and Laravel — chosen based on your project needs and budget.
Can you manage my Amazon PPC campaigns?
Yes. Our Amazon specialists handle keyword research, campaign structure, bid management and weekly optimization to maximize ROI.
How do you report progress?
100% transparency: you get regular updates, dashboards and detailed monthly reports on all metrics that matter.
What are your payment terms?
Typically 50% upfront and 50% on delivery for projects, with flexible milestone plans for larger engagements. Training courses have simple one-time or installment options.
Do you provide ongoing support after delivery?
Yes — every project includes a support window, and we offer long-term maintenance and growth partnerships with SLA-backed 24/7 support.
Can you train my in-house team?
Definitely. Our Enterprise plan includes tailored training programs for your staff on any of our course topics.
How do I get started?
Fill in the contact form below, tap any WhatsApp button, or email us at ayzsuccesshub@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few hours.
What makes AYZ Success Hub different?
We combine professional services with a training academy — real practitioners teach and execute, giving you both done-for-you results and the knowledge to sustain them.
